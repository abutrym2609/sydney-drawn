    About the project

    Hello,
    neighbour.

    A creative project by
    Anastasia Butrym

    I'm Ana. I'm glad you're here. Whether Sydney has been your home for years or you're still finding your feet, I'd love to show you around.

    You probably know the feeling: a street becomes familiar, someone remembers your coffee order, and a little piece of the city starts to feel like yours. That's the change I'm exploring in Sydney: From Space to Place—how the spaces around us become places we care about.

    Sydney keeps surprising me. Cross a bridge, turn a corner, or get off at a different stop, and you can find yourself in another little city. Each suburb has its own rhythm, memories and idea of a good day. I'd like you to meet them, one drawing at a time.

    Let me tell you what I found.

    Before I draw a neighbourhood, I listen to what people say about living there: the things they love, the things that drive them mad, the places they miss and the changes they're hoping for. I look into the history of its streets and buildings, too. An ordinary-looking corner can have a wonderful story to tell.

    You might recognise some of these stories. You might remember the same place quite differently. That's part of what makes a neighbourhood interesting: we share the streets, but each of us brings a different life to them.

    Shall we look a little closer?

    Here, Bondi spills out of a blue iMac, Balmain sits inside an iron ship, and Chippendale fits into a jacket pocket. There's a reason for each of those strange choices. Bondi gave Apple's Bondi Blue its name; Balmain's shipyards helped shape its working life; Chippendale brings homes, gardens, art and dinner wonderfully close together.

    As each picture appears, you can read about the details and why I drew them that way. Take your time. You may spot a familiar building first, then notice a small figure or a hidden piece of history that changes how you see the whole drawing.

    Start somewhere that means something to you.

    Look up your suburb, a place you used to live, or somewhere you've always wondered about. I hope you find something that makes you smile in recognition—and something that tempts you to go exploring. I'm still discovering Sydney, too. It's lovely to have your company.
