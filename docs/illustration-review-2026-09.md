# Sydney: From Space to Place — illustration and story review

Reviewed 7 September 2026. Creative project Claude + GPT + Gemini, curated by Anastasia Butrym.

All 106 current illustrations were visually inspected against their stories, including the accepted Glebe image with the new Sydney Fish Market. Every story now contains ten distinct illustrated details, an introduction and an individual slogan: 1,060 details in total. Narration uses We/us/our throughout; the title of Newtown's “I Have a Dream” mural is preserved.

The review covers correspondence between pictures and prose, significant omissions, geographical compression and targeted checks of changing projects and historical claims. It is an editorial review, not a claim that every historical statement has been independently verified against archival records. Dates and construction statuses below reflect the review date. The ten entries explain the strongest available elements of each existing picture; they do not turn an unpictured landmark into a pictured one.
